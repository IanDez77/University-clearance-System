Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the University Clearance System.

1. What is the University Clearance System?

The University Clearance System allows students to complete and track their university clearance process online.

2. How do I start my clearance process?

Log in to your student account and follow the instructions provided on your clearance dashboard.

3. How can I check my clearance status?

Log in to your account and check your dashboard to view the status of your clearance requests.

4. What happens if my clearance is rejected?

Check the feedback provided by the responsible department and complete any outstanding requirements before submitting again.

5. What should I do if I experience technical problems?

Contact the system administrator or visit the Help Desk for assistance.

6. Who can use the system?

Authorized students and university staff can use the system according to their assigned roles and permissions.

7. What if I forget my password?

Use the password reset option on the login page, if available, or contact the system administrator for assistance.
