Clearance Guide

Follow these steps to complete your clearance process efficiently.

Step 1: Log In

Access the University Clearance System by logging in with your student credentials.

Step 2: Review Requirements

Check the list of clearance requirements specific to your department and program.

Step 3: Submit Requests

Submit your clearance requests through the system, ensuring all necessary documents are attached.

Step 4: Track Status

Monitor the status of your clearance requests on your dashboard and respond to any feedback promptly.

Step 5: Finalize Clearance

Once all requirements are met and approved, finalize your clearance and download any necessary confirmation documents.
